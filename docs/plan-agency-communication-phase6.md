# Agency platform v2: Phase 6 plan, communication and enforcement

Phase 6 of the "Agent Seats, exclusivity, Platform Identity, client protection, unified Terms" spec. It builds on phase 5 (`docs/walkthrough-agency-client-protection-phase5.md`). The spec's delivery plan puts platform messaging with contact-detail detection, the interview request flow, the email alias model, bypass reporting and case management, and the dashboards here. One foundation is still missing: opportunities and submissions (old A4 and A5) were never built, and the spec's feature 12, the agency Opportunities tab, the client Opportunities tab, the "pending submissions withdrawn on exit" exit action, and the agency reports all depend on them. This plan therefore carries the minimum opportunity and submission flow too, and section 8 asks whether to ship it in this phase or split it out.

## 1. What exists and what this phase adds

| Area | Today | Phase 6 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Messaging | `InterviewMessage` threads live only on interview requests; `scanMessage` detects contact details and rate talk; contact details hold the message for Sales review (hold-only); authors are labelled "Hirewise", display name, or "Client". | `MessageThread` and `Message` for every pre-placement conversation (interview requests, opportunities, submissions, Partner Team enquiries), always Client ↔ Hirewise ↔ Partner Team or agent; a `messaging.contactDetails` setting chooses `MASK` (redacted copy delivered, original kept for staff) or `BLOCK` (held, as today); every detection raises a flag; URLs and meeting links join the detector. Author labels use the client label and the Partner Team name. The interview thread keeps working on the new tables. |
| Interviews | Clients request interviews from the shortlist; Sales schedules with a pasted link or the configured provider; any URL is accepted. | "Request Interview" from a candidate, a shortlist, a Partner Team page, or a submission; Sales schedules with requested windows and participants; the meeting link must come from the configured provider or match `meetings.allowedHosts` (default Hirewise-owned domains and the provider's); agency Calendly, Zoom, Meet, or email addresses are refused and flagged. Agency owners see their agents' interviews, read-only. |
| Email aliases | None. Outbound email only (SMTP). | `EmailAlias {alias, ownerType, ownerId, routingTarget, active}` with `talent####@hirewiseconnect.com` and `team####@` allocation, shown on client-facing views instead of any real address. Inbound routing is designed (`/api/inbound/email` webhook contract, signature, thread matching) and shipped behind `email.inboundEnabled = false` because the SMTP provider has no inbound route today; the model, allocation, and display ship now. |
| Bypass reporting | `Incident` (staff-created: rate discussion, off-platform contact, direct hire attempt) with evidence and suspension. | `BypassReport` cases raised by clients, agency owners, agents, and staff from threads, profiles, Partner Team pages, and a menu entry; statuses Reported → Under Review → Warning Issued / Restricted / Suspended → Closed; evidence attachments; assignment; decision notes; full audit; never an automatic suspension. ## 2. Schema (one additive migration plus a data backfill)

- `MessageThread {id, kind INTERVIEW_REQUEST / OPPORTUNITY / SUBMISSION / PARTNER_ENQUIRY / SUPPORT, subjectType, subjectId, clientId?, agencyId?, agentProfileId?, status OPEN / CLOSED, assignedStaffUserId?, createdAt, closedAt?}` and `Message {id, threadId, authorUserId, authorRole, body (delivered text), originalBody? (only when masked, staff-only), visibleTo, heldForReview, maskedAt?, releasedById?, releasedAt?, blockedAt?, findings String[], createdAt}`. `InterviewMessage` rows are migrated into threads of kind `INTERVIEW_REQUEST` and the old table is kept read-only for history.
- `Interview` gains `requestedWindows Json?`, `participants Json?` (names and roles, no emails), and `linkSource PROVIDER / STAFF_PASTED`.
- `EmailAlias {id, alias @unique, ownerType AGENT_PROFILE / AGENCY / CLIENT, ownerId, routingTarget (user id or thread rule), active, createdAt}` plus a sequence for the numeric part.
- `BypassReport {id, reporterUserId, reporterRole, reportedUserId?, reportedAgencyId?, reportedClientId?, relatedType?, relatedId?, description, evidenceDocumentIds String[], status REPORTED / UNDER_REVIEW / WARNING_ISSUED / RESTRICTED / SUSPENDED / CLOSED, assignedToUserId?, decisionNotes?, decidedById?, decidedAt?, createdAt, updatedAt}` with an append-only `BypassReportEvent` history.
- `Opportunity {id, requirementId, clientId, title, roleSummary, maskedClientLabel, skills, schedule, timezone, startDate, headcount, status DRAFT / OPEN / INTERVIEWING / FILLED / CLOSED, publishedById, publishedAt, closedAt, visibility ALL_AGENCIES / SELECTED_AGENCIES / DIRECT_ONLY}` and `OpportunityAgency {opportunityId, agencyId}` for selected visibility.
- `Submission {id, opportunityId, agentProfileId, agencyId?, submittedById, note, status PENDING_REVIEW / APPROVED_FOR_CLIENT / DECLINED / WITHDRAWN / INTERVIEWING / SELECTED / NOT_SELECTED, reviewNote, reviewedById, reviewedAt, withdrawnReason, createdAt}` with a partial unique index on one open submission per agent and opportunity.
- Settings: `messaging {contactDetails: "MASK", flagRateTalk: true}`, `meetings {allowedHosts: ["meet.hirewise.example", "zoom.us", "meet.google.com", "teams.microsoft.com"], requireProvider: false}`, `email {inboundEnabled: false, aliasDomain: "hirewiseconnect.com"}`, `bypass {autoRestrictOnReport: false}` (fixed false, documented), `opportunities {requireActiveCredentials: true, maxSubmissionsPerAgency: 5}`.
- Backfill: one thread per existing interview request; aliases allocated for approved agents and active agencies; nothing else.

## 3. Flows

- **Messaging**: `postMessage(thread, body)` runs `scanMessage` plus the phase 5 redaction patterns; with `MASK`, the delivered body is the redacted text, the original is kept on `originalBody` for staff, a flag is raised, and the author sees "contact details were removed"; with `BLOCK`, today's hold-and-review flow runs. Staff reply from the thread; relays stay mediated. Thread participants: the client's contacts, the Partner Team owner (for agency agents), the agent, and the assigned Sales or account manager. Closed threads refuse posts.
- **Interviews**: the request records windows and the candidate source (shortlist, Partner Team page, or submission); Sales schedules; `assertMeetingLinkAllowed(url)` rejects links outside the provider and the allow-list with a clear error and a `MEETING_LINK_REFUSED` flag; calendar invites carry only the Hirewise-owned link. The agent's and the client's views stay label-only per phase 5.
- **Aliases**: `aliasFor(ownerType, ownerId)` allocates once; client views show `contactAlias` for a candidate or team; outbound notifications set `Reply-To` to the alias when inbound is enabled, otherwise to the Hirewise address. The inbound webhook contract is documented in `docs/adr/00x-email-aliases.md` and implemented as a route that verifies a signature, finds the alias, and posts into the matching thread, disabled by the setting.
- **Bypass reports**: "Report Direct Contact / Bypass Attempt" opens a form with the related subject prefilled; the case is created with status Reported, staff are notified, and nothing else changes automatically. Staff move it through the statuses; Warning Issued notifies the reported party with the decision note; Restricted applies a permission override or availability hold with a reason and duration; Suspended calls the existing `suspendUser` with the case as reason; Closed records the outcome. Every step is audited and appended to the case history.
- **Opportunities**: Sales publishes an opportunity from a requirement (masked label from phase 5, headcount, schedule, skills); eligible agencies see it under Opportunities; the owner submits an agent (eligibility checked server-side: active membership, approved profile, active credentials, availability, no open submission, agency cap); direct talent may submit themselves; Hirewise reviews; approved submissions appear to the client as candidates on the opportunity with the client-safe card; the client requests interviews from there, which creates the interview request with those candidates; selection flows into placements as today. Exit: open submissions of the leaving agent are withdrawn with the reason and Hirewise notified (`AGENCY_EXIT_COMPLETED` handler).
- **Dashboards**: tenant-scoped aggregates per agency (members, available, in training, submissions by status, interviews, placements, certification counts) in `agency-report.service.ts`; the client dashboard gains Opportunities, Messages, and Partner Teams tiles; the staff dashboard gains bypass cases, submissions pending review, and documents waiting; Partner Team `performanceRating` = share of placements reaching ACTIVE over selected submissions, null below five data points.

## 4. Server

- New: `thread.service.ts` (threads, posting with mask or block, release and block, participants), `alias.service.ts`, `bypass.service.ts`, `opportunity.service.ts` (publish, visibility, eligibility, submissions, review, withdrawal on exit), `agency-report.service.ts`, `meeting-link.ts` (allow-list check), repositories for each, views `thread.views.ts`, `bypass.views.ts`, `opportunity.views.ts` (client, agency, agent, staff audiences with the phase 5 forbidden-key lists extended).
- Changed: `message.service.ts` becomes a thin wrapper over threads for the interview pages; `interview.service.scheduleInterviews` validates links and records windows and participants; `handlers.ts` adds thread, bypass, submission, and alias events and the exit withdrawal; `dashboard.service.ts` counters; `partner.views.ts` rating.
- Permissions (new, proposed): `thread.moderate` (release, block, close threads), `bypass.review` (work cases), `opportunity.manage` (publish, close, review submissions). Existing `agency.opportunities.read`, `agency.submissions.create`, `agency.reports.read`, `interview.coordinate`, `interview.schedule`, `flag.review`, `incident.write` are reused.
- Audit: `MESSAGE_MASKED`, `THREAD_CLOSED`, `MEETING_LINK_REFUSED`, `ALIAS_ALLOCATED`, `BYPASS_REPORTED`, `BYPASS_STATUS_CHANGED`, `OPPORTUNITY_PUBLISHED`, `OPPORTUNITY_CLOSED`, `SUBMISSION_CREATED`, `SUBMISSION_REVIEWED`, `SUBMISSION_WITHDRAWN`. Events: `MESSAGE_POSTED` (moved to threads), `MESSAGE_MASKED`, `BYPASS_REPORTED`, `BYPASS_DECIDED`, `OPPORTUNITY_PUBLISHED`, `SUBMISSION_CREATED`, `SUBMISSION_REVIEWED`, `SUBMISSION_WITHDRAWN`.

## 5. UI

- Shared `MessageThread` on interview requests, opportunities, submissions, and a Messages page per role; the "contact details removed" notice; staff moderation controls.
- Client: Opportunities (open requirements published as opportunities, candidates per opportunity, Request Interview), Messages, Report Bypass Attempt (menu entry and buttons on threads, candidates, and Partner Team pages).
- Agency: Opportunities (open, eligible agents, submit, submitted, interviews, placements), Reports (team performance, training, placement), interviews read-only, Report Bypass Attempt.
- Agent: Opportunities (direct talent only), Interviews, Assignments, Performance, Report Bypass Attempt; no submission button while an agency membership is active (the agency submits).
- Staff: Opportunities and submissions queue, message flags and held or masked messages, Bypass cases with history and actions, alias list, dashboard counters.

## 6. Tests

Masking and blocking per setting with flags and audit; staff see originals, others never; thread participation and tenant scope (agency B cannot read agency A's threads); meeting link allow-list and refusal flag; alias allocation uniqueness and display on client views (no real address anywhere, extending the phase 5 leak sweep); bypass case lifecycle with no automatic suspension, evidence, restrictions through overrides, audit trail; opportunity visibility, eligibility rules (inactive credentials excluded, no second submission, agency cap, exclusivity: an agency agent cannot self-submit), review, client candidates, interview request from an opportunity, withdrawal on exit; agency report aggregates tenant-scoped; forbidden keys on every new view.

## 7. Decisions to confirm

1. **Contact-detail handling** defaults to `MASK` (the message is delivered with "[removed]" and a flag) rather than today's hold-and-review; staff can switch to `BLOCK` in settings.
2. **Meeting links**: the configured provider is preferred; pasted links are allowed only for hosts on the allow-list, which defaults to the provider's domain plus Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams. Agency-owned or unknown hosts are refused and flagged.
3. **Email aliases** ship as model, allocation, and display only. Inbound routing is designed and disabled until the email provider supports it.
4. **Bypass restrictions** apply through existing permission overrides and `suspendUser`; no case changes anything automatically, including repeated reports.
5. **Opportunities are included in this phase** as the minimum flow in section 3, because the agency Opportunities tab, the client Opportunities tab, the exit action, and the reports cannot be built without them.
6. **Submission eligibility** requires an active membership for agency agents, an approved profile, active credentials only, availability AVAILABLE or AVAILABLE_SOON, and at most `opportunities.maxSubmissionsPerAgency` open submissions per agency and opportunity (default 5).
7. **Performance rating** is placements reaching ACTIVE divided by selected submissions, shown only after five data points; everything else on the Partner Team view stays as in phase 5.
8. **New permissions** `thread.moderate`, `bypass.review`, and `opportunity.manage`, granted to ADMIN and SALES (SALES already coordinates interviews and works clients).

## 8. Scope question

Decision 5 makes this the largest phase so far. The alternative is two pull requests: 6a (messaging, interviews, aliases, bypass reports, staff dashboard counters) and 6b (opportunities, submissions, exit withdrawal, agency and client dashboards, reports, performance rating). The work is the same; the split only changes when the first part reaches production. Say "approved" for one phase, or "approved, split" for 6a then 6b.
