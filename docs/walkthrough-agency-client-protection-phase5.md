# Agency platform v2, phase 5: client protection

Phase 5 of the "Agent Seats, exclusivity, Platform Identity, client protection, unified Terms" spec (plan and the approved decisions 1 to 8: `docs/plan-agency-client-protection-phase5.md`). Everything a client receives now goes through allow-list serializers that know nothing about the agency behind a candidate, use a client label instead of the agent's display name, redact contact routes from free text, and serve only documents Hirewise has cleared. Identities cross that boundary only through explicit, revocable disclosure grants.

## What changed

| Area | Delivered |
| --- | --- |
| Partner Team identity | `Agency.clientFacingCode` (`HWC-001`, from a sequence, unique, assigned by `assignPartnerIdentityTx` at approval and direct creation, backfilled for existing agencies), `clientFacingName` (default "Hirewise Certified Partner Team HWC-001"), `clientFacingSpecializations`. Hirewise edits name and specializations with `partner.identity.manage`; the name may not contain the real agency name. Agency users read it on Settings and any attempt to write a `clientFacing*` key through the profile update is a `ForbiddenError`. Private fields `legalName`, `addressLine`, `registrationDetails`, `billingEmail` are owner-editable and Hirewise-visible, never client-visible. |
| Client label | `AgentProfile.clientLabel` ("Maria C.": first name plus last initial of the legal name, falling back to the display name), set at registration and on fresh profiles, kept in sync by `updatePersonal` unless staff pinned it (`setClientLabel`, `clientLabelOverride`). Every client surface uses `name` (the label, or the legal name through a grant); `displayName` is in `FORBIDDEN_FOR_CLIENT`. The public certificate page shows the label. |
| Redaction | `src/lib/client-redaction.ts`: `redactForClient` removes emails, phones, links and bare domains, social handles, and messaging hints, and replaces agency names with the Partner Team name. It runs inside `toCandidateClientView` and `toCandidateCardView` for the `CLIENT` audience (headline, summary, experience and portfolio descriptions). |
| Client serializers | `toCandidateClientView(a, { audience, legalName?, clientSafe?, agencyNames? })` and `toCandidateCardView(a, { audience, clientSafe? })`: `name`, `partnerTeam {code, name} | null`, `identityDisclosed`, redacted text, media only with an approved client-safe row, portfolio only as approved documents (no raw `url`), `documents` (generated profile and certificate PDFs). Staff get the display name and unredacted text through `audience: "STAFF"`; `searchCandidates`, `getCandidateForClient` (with `previewAsClient`), shortlist, compare, recommendations, and recently viewed pick the audience from the actor. `toPartnerTeamClientView` is the only agency shape a client receives. |
| Client-safe documents | `ClientSafeDocument` per source item (`VIDEO`, `RECORDING`, `PORTFOLIO`, `CERTIFICATION`, `PROFILE`) with statuses `PENDING`, `AUTO_SANITIZED`, `NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW`, `APPROVED_FOR_CLIENT`, `REJECTED`. Rows are created by the outbox handlers for `PROFILE_REVIEWED`, `MEDIA_REVIEWED`, and `CERTIFICATION_APPROVED`, and by `syncClientSafeDocuments` in the worker's maintenance pass (also covers portfolio items and anything missed). Generated profile and certificate PDFs are built from the redacted client view with `renderSimplePdf`, stored under `client-safe/…`, and approved automatically when `clientProtection.autoApproveGenerated` is on; video, audio, and uploaded or linked portfolio items wait for a reviewer (`media.review`) under Media review. Clients open items only through `clientSafeDocumentUrl` or the media gate in `mediaDownloadUrl`, both of which require an `APPROVED_FOR_CLIENT` row. Originals are never altered. |
| Disclosure grants | `DisclosureGrant {scope AGENT_IDENTITY / AGENCY_IDENTITY / CLIENT_IDENTITY, clientId, agentProfileId?, agencyId?, grantedBy STAFF / SYSTEM, reason, interviewId?, revokedAt…}` with one active grant per scope, client, and subject. Staff grant and revoke with `disclosure.manage`; the system grants at interview scheduling per the `disclosure` setting (agent identity and client identity for direct talent by default, agency agents manual) and both identities when a placement is created. Views consult grants: the client's interview requests and placements show the legal name only with a grant; the agent's requests and placements show the company only with a grant and otherwise a masked label ("Healthcare client, United States"); the Partner Team view adds `disclosed.agencyName` only with an agency grant. Existing scheduled interviews and placements were backfilled as system grants. |
| UI | Client: Partner Teams list and detail (`/partners`, `/partners/[code]`), candidate cards and profile with the Partner Team line, disclosed badge, and a Documents card; interviews and placements by label. Agency: Settings shows the read-only client-facing identity and the private identity fields. Agent: "Clients see you as …" on My profile. Staff: side-by-side identities with edit and agency grants on the agency page; "What clients see" on the talent page (label form, client-safe documents, grants, preview as client); the client-safe queue under Media review. |
| Settings | `clientProtection {autoApproveGenerated}` and `disclosure {agentIdentityAt, clientIdentityForDirectTalent, clientIdentityForAgencyAgents}`. `identityDisclosureLevel` is superseded and kept for history. |

## Where the rules are enforced

- Client-facing names come from `clientNameFor` (interview views) and the `name` field of the candidate views; the display name never enters a client projection, and `tests/unit/views.test.ts` plus the leak sweep in `tests/integration/client-protection.test.ts` prove it.
- Agency identity reaches clients only through `partner.views.ts`; the agency record is never passed to a client page. The leak sweep asserts that the agency's real name, website, the agent's legal name, email, phone, LinkedIn URL, and handle appear in no client response (search, candidate, shortlist, compare, Partner Teams, interview requests, placements, export) and that no `FORBIDDEN_FOR_CLIENT` key appears.
- `assertNoClientFacingKeys` guards the agency profile update; `updatePartnerIdentity` requires `partner.identity.manage`.
- Clients open media and documents only through `clientMayOpenMedia` and `clientSafeDocumentUrl`; both require `APPROVED_FOR_CLIENT`.
- Grants are read through `disclosure.service` lookups; nothing infers disclosure from a status any more (`DISCLOSE_COMPANY_FROM` remains only for the staff view).

Existing tests updated: client views use `name`, clients need client-safe rows for media, the certificate page shows the label, and the placement test expects the disclosed legal name.
