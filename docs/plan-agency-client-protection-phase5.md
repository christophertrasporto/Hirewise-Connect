# Agency platform v2: Phase 5 plan, client protection

Phase 5 of the "Agent Seats, exclusivity, Platform Identity, client protection, unified Terms" spec. It builds on phase 4 (`docs/walkthrough-agency-credentials-phase4.md`). Opportunities and submissions (old A4 and A5) are still unbuilt, so clients meet agency agents today only through the marketplace (search, candidate profiles, shortlists), interview requests, placements, notifications, and the client export. This phase makes every one of those surfaces safe, adds the Partner Team identity, client-safe documents with a review queue, and disclosure grants. The client-facing opportunity flow itself arrives with Phase 6.

## 1. What exists and what this phase adds

| Area | Today | Phase 5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agency identity | `Agency` has the real identity (name, logo, contact email and phone, website, country, city). No client-facing name or code. Clients never see agencies. | `clientFacingCode` ("HWC-018", system-generated on approval, unique, immutable), `clientFacingName` (default "Hirewise Certified Partner Team HWC-018", Admin-only edit), `clientFacingSpecializations` (Admin-curated), private fields `legalName`, `addressLine`, `registrationDetails`, `billingEmail` (owner-editable, Admin-visible, never client-visible). Backfill codes for existing agencies. |
| Agent identity toward clients | `displayName` is agent-chosen free text, often a full name, and reaches clients on cards, profiles, interview requests, placements, emails, the export, and the public certificate page. Legal name is revealed to the client from `SCHEDULED`. | `clientLabel` ("Maria S.") derived from the legal name at approval and kept in sync, Admin-overridable. Client surfaces use only `clientLabel`; `displayName` joins `FORBIDDEN_FOR_CLIENT`. Full name reaches a client only through an active `DisclosureGrant`. |
| Free text and links | `summary`, `headline`, experience and portfolio descriptions, and `Portfolio.url` pass to clients unchanged. `CONTACT_INFO_IN_PROFILE` only flags. | `redactForClient(text)` runs inside every client serializer: emails, phones, URLs, social handles, and messaging hints become "[removed]", known agency names become the Partner Team name. Portfolio links are withheld from clients unless the media reviewer marks the link client-safe. |
| Documents and media | Clients get signed URLs for approved videos and recordings; the uploaded résumé is already hidden (`hasResume` only); certificates are Hirewise-generated PDFs showing the agent's name. No review for client safety as such. | `ClientSafeDocument` per source item with statuses PENDING, AUTO_SANITIZED, NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW, APPROVED_FOR_CLIENT, REJECTED. Clients are served only `APPROVED_FOR_CLIENT` rows, resolved through one service function. Originals are never altered. Staff review queue under Media review. |
| Disclosure | Implicit: the client sees the legal name at `SCHEDULED`; the candidate sees the client company name at `SCHEDULED` (`DISCLOSE_COMPANY_FROM`). | `DisclosureGrant` with scopes AGENT_IDENTITY, AGENCY_IDENTITY, CLIENT_IDENTITY; granted by staff or by the system at interview scheduling per settings; revocable; audited. Views consult grants, not statuses. |
| Client navigation | Talent, Shortlist, Interviews, Placements, Requirements, Billing. | Plus Partner Teams: list and detail built from the Partner Team allow-list. Candidate cards and profiles say "Represented by Hirewise Certified Partner Team HWC-018" or "Hirewise Direct talent". |
| Agency settings | Private profile editable by the owner. | A read-only "Client-facing identity" card; the update action rejects any attempt to write client-facing fields (test). |
| Staff | Agency detail shows the real identity. | Real identity and client-facing identity side by side with Admin edit of name and specializations; disclosure grants (grant and revoke) on the agency, talent, and client pages; the client-safe document queue; a "preview as client" of any approved candidate. |

## 2. Schema (one additive migration plus a data backfill)

- `Agency` gains `clientFacingCode String? @unique`, `clientFacingName String?`, `clientFacingSpecializations String[]`, `legalName String?`, `addressLine String?`, `registrationDetails Json?`, `billingEmail String?`. A Postgres sequence `agency_client_code_seq` feeds the code (`HWC-` plus three or more digits). The data migration assigns codes and default names to existing agencies ordered by `joinedAt`, then the schema migration's follow-up sets the columns NOT NULL for `clientFacingCode` and `clientFacingName` (two steps so the backfill happens first).
- `AgentProfile` gains `clientLabel String?` and `clientLabelOverride Boolean @default(false)`. Backfill: first word of `privateContact.fullLegalName` plus last initial; when no private contact exists, first word of `displayName` plus last initial; a single word stays as is.
- `Portfolio` gains `clientLinkApproved Boolean @default(false)`.
- `enum ClientSafeDocumentType { RESUME, PROFILE, CERTIFICATE, PORTFOLIO, VOICE_INTRO, VIDEO_INTRO }`, `enum SanitizationStatus { PENDING, AUTO_SANITIZED, NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW, APPROVED_FOR_CLIENT, REJECTED }`.
- `ClientSafeDocument {id, agentProfileId, type, sourceType (VIDEO, RECORDING, PORTFOLIO, CERTIFICATION, RESUME, PROFILE), sourceId, originalStorageKey, sanitizedStorageKey?, status, findings Json (what the scan found, no raw contact values), autoNote?, reviewedById?, reviewedAt?, reviewNote?, createdAt, updatedAt}`, unique on `(sourceType, sourceId, originalStorageKey)` so a re-upload creates a fresh row and the old approval does not carry over.
- `enum DisclosureScope { AGENT_IDENTITY, AGENCY_IDENTITY, CLIENT_IDENTITY }`, `enum DisclosureGrantor { STAFF, SYSTEM }`.
- `DisclosureGrant {id, scope, clientId, agentProfileId?, agencyId?, grantedById?, grantedBy DisclosureGrantor, reason, interviewId?, grantedAt, expiresAt?, revokedAt?, revokedById?, revokeReason?}` with a partial unique index on the active grant per `(scope, clientId, agentProfileId, agencyId)`.
- Settings: `clientProtection { autoApproveGenerated: true, portfolioLinks: "REVIEW" }` and `disclosure { agentIdentityAt: "INTERVIEW_SCHEDULED", clientIdentityForAgencyAgents: "MANUAL", clientIdentityForDirectTalent: "INTERVIEW_SCHEDULED" }`. `identityDisclosureLevel` stays as the ceiling: with `DISPLAY_NAME` the system never auto-grants.
- `FORBIDDEN_FOR_CLIENT` gains `displayName`, `name` (agency), `logoKey`, `website`, `contactEmail`, `contactPhone`, `legalName`, `registrationDetails`, `billingEmail`, `storageKey`, `url`, `meetingProvider`, `meetingExternalId`. `FORBIDDEN_FOR_AGENCY` gains `companyName` (until a CLIENT_IDENTITY grant) and `clientFacingCode` of other agencies is simply never queried (tenant scope).

## 3. Flows

- **Partner Team identity**: approval of an agency application (and `agency.create_direct`) allocates the next code and the default name inside the same transaction and audits `PARTNER_IDENTITY_ASSIGNED`. Admin edits name and specializations under Staff → Agencies → [agency] (`PARTNER_IDENTITY_UPDATED`). The owner's settings form and `updateAgencyProfile` strip and reject `clientFacing*` keys with `ForbiddenError`.
- **Client label**: set when the profile is approved, recomputed whenever the private contact's legal name changes unless `clientLabelOverride` is true; staff can override on the talent page (`CLIENT_LABEL_SET`). The public certificate verification page shows the client label, never the legal or display name.
- **Client serializers**: `toCandidateClientView` and `toCandidateCardView` emit `clientLabel`, `partnerTeam: {code, name} | null` (null for Hirewise Direct and independent talent), redacted text, portfolio items without `url` unless `clientLinkApproved`, and `legalName` only when an active AGENT_IDENTITY grant exists for the requesting client (passed in by the service; serializers never query). `toRequestClientView`, `toPlacementView("CLIENT")`, the client export, and client notifications switch to the client label. New `toPartnerTeamClientView(agency, stats)` is the only thing a client endpoint may receive about an agency: `code, name, specializations, qualifiedProfessionals, teamSize, availableNow, inTraining, trainingCompletionPct, certifications [{name, count}], avgYearsExperience, performanceRating (null until Phase 6 reports), memberSince (month and year)`. With an active AGENCY_IDENTITY grant the service adds `disclosed: {name, website, contactEmail}`.
- **Masked client label toward agency agents** (phase 1 decision f, confirmed here): the agent view of an interview request for an agency agent shows `clientLabel` built from industry and country ("Healthcare client, United States") until a CLIENT_IDENTITY grant; Hirewise Direct and independent talent keep the company name from `SCHEDULED`, which now happens by a system grant so it is visible and revocable.
- **Client-safe documents**: a row is created when a source item becomes eligible (video, recording, or portfolio file approved by media review; certification approved; profile approved for the generated PROFILE PDF). Auto-sanitization in this phase covers what can be done without new dependencies: PROFILE and CERTIFICATE PDFs are generated from allow-listed, redacted data with `renderSimplePdf` and marked AUTO_SANITIZED (auto-approved when `autoApproveGenerated` is on); the uploaded résumé is never served, the generated PROFILE PDF stands in for it (type RESUME is reserved for a later text-extraction phase); VOICE_INTRO, VIDEO_INTRO, and uploaded PORTFOLIO files go to NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW with the finding "audio, video, or binary content cannot be scanned". Staff approve or reject in the queue (`CLIENT_SAFE_DOCUMENT_REVIEWED`); approval of a media original sets `sanitizedStorageKey` to the original key, which is served read-only. `mediaDownloadUrl` for a CLIENT actor resolves only through an APPROVED_FOR_CLIENT row; every other path returns `NotFoundError`.
- **Disclosure grants**: staff grant and revoke with a reason from the client, talent, and agency pages (`DISCLOSURE_GRANTED`, `DISCLOSURE_REVOKED`, event `DISCLOSURE_CHANGED` notifies the affected parties' Hirewise manager, not the client). Interview scheduling creates system grants per the `disclosure` settings. Views and services read grants through `disclosureService.activeGrants(db, clientId, …)`.
- **Partner Teams for clients**: `/partners` lists active agencies with at least one approved, available-for-marketplace agent; `/partners/[code]` shows the team view and its approved candidates as cards. Both go through `assertMarketplaceAccess` like the talent pages.

## 4. Server

- New: `partner.service.ts` (code allocation, identity reads for clients and staff, Admin edit), `disclosure.service.ts` (grants, auto-grant at scheduling, lookups), `client-safe-document.service.ts` (row creation hooks, generation, review queue, client resolution), `src/server/views/partner.views.ts`, `src/lib/client-redaction.ts` (pure `redactForClient`, reuses the patterns in `message-filter.ts` plus agency-name replacement), repositories `partner.repository.ts`, `disclosure.repository.ts`, `client-safe-document.repository.ts`.
- Changed: `agency-application.service.approve` and `agency.service.createDirect` allocate the identity; `agency.service.updateProfile` rejects client-facing keys; `agent.service` approval and private-contact updates maintain `clientLabel`; `media.service.mediaDownloadUrl` and `reviewMedia` route clients through client-safe rows; `certification.service` approval generates the client-safe certificate; `interview.service.scheduleInterviews` creates system grants; `interview.views`, `agent.views`, `export.service`, `handlers.ts` client notifications, and the certificate verification page use the client label.
- Permissions (new, proposed because the spec names these capabilities and none existing fits): `disclosure.manage` (staff: grant and revoke), `partner.identity.manage` (staff: edit client-facing name and specializations). Client-safe document review reuses `media.review`. Agency owner reads of their own client-facing identity reuse `agency.settings.manage`.
- Audit: `PARTNER_IDENTITY_ASSIGNED`, `PARTNER_IDENTITY_UPDATED`, `CLIENT_LABEL_SET`, `CLIENT_SAFE_DOCUMENT_CREATED`, `CLIENT_SAFE_DOCUMENT_REVIEWED`, `DISCLOSURE_GRANTED`, `DISCLOSURE_REVOKED`. Events: `CLIENT_SAFE_DOCUMENT_NEEDS_REVIEW` (task for staff), `CLIENT_SAFE_DOCUMENT_REVIEWED` (notify the agent), `DISCLOSURE_CHANGED`.

## 5. UI

- Client: Partner Teams list and detail; candidate cards and profile show the client label and the Partner Team line; "Full name disclosed by Hirewise" badge when a grant exists; documents section lists only client-safe items.
- Agency portal: Settings gains the read-only client-facing identity card and the private identity fields; Team member rows show the client label next to the display name so owners know what clients see.
- Agent: profile page shows "Clients see you as Maria S." and the client-safe status of each media item.
- Staff: Agencies → [agency] side-by-side identities with edit; Media review gains a "Client-safe documents" tab; Talent → [agent] shows client label override, client-safe documents, and grants; Clients → [client] lists grants with grant and revoke; "Preview as client" on the talent page renders the client view.

## 6. Tests

- Client surface sweep: a fixture agency named "Villanueva Virtual Teams" with an agent whose legal name, display name, email, phone, LinkedIn URL, and agency website appear in free text; every client-facing service output (search, candidate, cards, shortlist compare, interview request, placement, export, partner team list and detail, client notification payloads) is serialized and asserted to contain none of those strings and none of the `FORBIDDEN_FOR_CLIENT` keys. The same sweep with an AGENT_IDENTITY grant shows the legal name on that candidate only; with an AGENCY_IDENTITY grant the real agency name appears only in `disclosed`.
- Partner identity: allocated on approval and on direct creation, unique and sequential, owner update rejects client-facing keys, Admin edit audited, agency B cannot read agency A's identity.
- Client label: derivation cases, sync on legal-name change, override sticks, certificate page uses it.
- Redaction: unit table for emails, phones, URLs, handles, agency names, and safe text left intact.
- Client-safe documents: rows created on each eligibility event, generated PDFs auto-approved per setting, media goes to manual review, clients receive URLs only for approved rows, re-upload resets approval, originals untouched (storage key unchanged, bytes unchanged).
- Disclosure: system grants at scheduling follow the settings; `DISPLAY_NAME` ceiling blocks auto-grants; revocation hides the name again; the masked client label for agency agents versus the company name for direct talent.
- Projection tests: forbidden keys absent from every client and agency view, including the new ones.

## 7. Decisions to confirm

1. **Client label source**: first name plus last initial derived from the legal name at approval (fallback display name), with a staff override. The agent does not choose it.
2. **Display name becomes forbidden for clients** across the board, including the public certificate verification page and client emails. Existing clients will see labels change on next load.
3. **Portfolio links** are withheld from clients unless the media reviewer marks the link client-safe; existing approved items start unmarked, so links disappear for clients until re-reviewed.
4. **Auto-sanitization scope**: generated profile and certificate PDFs only; uploaded résumés are not served to clients in any form (the generated profile PDF replaces them); audio, video, and uploaded portfolio files always need manual approval. Text extraction from uploaded PDFs is deferred because it needs a new dependency.
5. **Existing approved media** is not re-reviewed: approved videos, recordings, and portfolio files get `NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW` rows, so clients lose access to them until staff approve. The alternative, grandfathering them as approved, is one flag in the data migration.
6. **Disclosure defaults**: agent identity auto-granted at interview scheduling (today's behavior, now explicit and revocable); client identity auto-granted at scheduling for Hirewise Direct and independent talent, manual for agency agents (phase 1 decision f); agency identity manual only.
7. **New permissions** `disclosure.manage` and `partner.identity.manage`, granted to ADMIN and the staff role that holds `agency.manage`.
8. **Phase 6 boundary**: meeting-link host restrictions, platform messaging outside interview threads, email aliases, bypass reports, and client opportunities stay in Phase 6.
